How the score is computed

score(work) = SUM over supporting engagements of
                  confidence x stance_weight

stance_weight:  praises 1.0   criticizes 0.8   neutral 0.6

Criticism is weighted below praise but well above nothing: a work attacking a concept you care about is still highly relevant to you. That is a judgement call, stated here so it can be disagreed with. Ranking is arithmetic, which is why it lives in Python rather than in OWL or SPARQL.
